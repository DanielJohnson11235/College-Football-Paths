How the tool decides

Top two teams by Big Ten record meet in Indianapolis. Ties are broken with the conference's published order: head-to-head, record against common conference opponents, record against the best common opponents in order of finish, then the combined conference win % of each team's opponents. The final step, the SportSource Analytics rating, can't be computed here, so those rare cases are split evenly. When a step knocks teams out of a multi-team tie, the survivors restart at head-to-head.

"Model odds" come from a simple power rating fit to the margins of all 54 Big Ten games played so far, with 2.5 points for home field. The Illinois at UCLA and Northwestern at Ohio State lines use published win probabilities. Odds are a rough guide. Each update runs 20,000 simulations of the unpicked games.

Tabs cover teams at 1% or better with no games picked: UCLA, Indiana, Oregon, Ohio State, Wisconsin, Iowa and USC. Illinois (under 1%), Minnesota and Northwestern still have long-shot paths and show up in the standings. "Most likely way in" is the single likeliest full set of results that sends the selected team to Indianapolis: favorites win every game except the fewest, likeliest upsets needed.
