How the tool decides

Two tiebreak steps can't be computed ahead of time. The SportSource Analytics rating isn't public until after the season, so the tool uses the current CFP ranking as a stand-in, then its own power rating for unranked teams. The SEC's capped scoring-margin step depends on final scores, so ties that reach it are split evenly. When a step knocks teams out of a multi-team tie, the survivors restart at head-to-head.

"Model odds" come from a simple power rating for each conference, fit to the margins of every conference game played so far, with 2.5 points for home field. Where a published win probability exists for this week's game, it's used instead. Odds are a rough guide. Each update runs 20,000 simulations of the unpicked games.
